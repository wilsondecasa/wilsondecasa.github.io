GADELO SPECIAL EDITION

Frozen Chosin

Dark roast, baked sweet potato, dalgona, toffee. A warm cup for the long road.

In December 1950, in the bitter cold of the Chosin Reservoir, U.S. Marines, soldiers of the U.S. Army, British Royal Marines, and Korean allies fought their way out against overwhelming odds. At Koto-ri, a faint star appeared one snowbound night — some called it a sign, some called it chance. This blend is our way of saying thank you: roasted slow and sweet, with notes of baked sweet potato and burnt-sugar toffee, made to be held with both hands on a cold morning.

OUR STORY

Roasted with intention, from the ground up.

GADELO Coffee Roasters is a small-batch roastery built on three things: quality green beans sourced carefully, creativity in how we blend and name every roast, and a quiet sense of purpose behind the whole operation.

Every blend — Fight Tonight, Uncle Sam, Roll Out, Indian Head, Freedom — is named with meaning, not marketing. We roast weekly in small batches so what ships to you is never far from the drum.

Quality Creativity Purpose
For the USFK Community

Roasted at Camp Humphreys. Shipped Across the Pacific.

GADELO roasts out of Camp Humphreys in Pyeongtaek and ships across the Armed Forces Pacific (APO AP) footprint — Korea, Japan, Okinawa, Guam, and Hawaii — plus the continental U.S. Every bag is roasted to order, so it's never far from the drum by the time it reaches you, wherever in the Pacific that is. Bulk orders for units and Spouses' Clubs, and bilingual support — wherever the mission takes you.
